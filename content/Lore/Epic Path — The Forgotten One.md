
You once had a place in the world.

A family. A home. A name that meant something.

You were not alone—you were loved.

Then one day, everything changed.

When you returned home, your family looked at you… as if you were a stranger.

No recognition. No warmth. No memory.

Your partner did not know your name. Your children did not know your face. Your own blood spoke to you with the distance reserved for outsiders.

Nothing else had changed.

The house was still lived in. Your life still existed.

Only **you** had been erased from it.

At first, you tried to explain. To remind them. To prove the truth.

It didn’t work.

Now, you’ve stopped trying to convince them.

Instead, you will find out **why**.

Was it magic? A curse? The will of something greater?

Or something worse—

Did someone decide you should be forgotten?

You will uncover the truth, no matter the cost.

And when you do…

You will decide whether to take your place back—

or burn everything that cast you out.

---

## Adventure Hook

There are forces in this world that shape memory, identity, and fate.

If you follow the threads of your own disappearance—

You may uncover something far greater than your own story.

---

## Heroic Tasks

- To uncover the truth behind why your family forgot you.  
- To reclaim your place in your family—or decide their fate.  
- To recover your family artifacts, which may hold the key to restoring what was lost. Pick one lesser and one greater artifact:

› **(Lesser) Ring of Protection**: once worn by your family—now they do not remember giving it to you.  
› **(Lesser) Amulet of Health**: engraved with a crest no one recognizes anymore.  
› **(Lesser) Ioun Stone (Awareness)**: a relic tied to your bloodline’s forgotten past.  

› **(Greater) Helm of Brilliance**: a symbol of rulership tied to a legacy that no longer acknowledges you.  
› **(Greater) Dancing Sword**: a weapon bound to your family’s founder—one of the few things that may still “know” you.  
› **(Greater) Rod of Absorption**: a relic of leadership—once yours by right.

### Blessing of Magic Resistance
You have advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
