
## Classification
Arcane Village  
Located in the [[Kingdom of Aethryss]]  

---

## Overview
Japuca is a small but well-known village within Aethryss, recognized for its long-standing tradition of producing mages.

Despite its modest size, it has participated in numerous historical events, contributing its own… distinctive approach to magic.

---

## 🟢 Level 1 — Common Knowledge

### Identity
Japuca is known as:

- A village of mages  
- A place of strong magical expression  
- A community defined by rivalry  

---

### Structure
Japuca is located within a valley and divided into two opposing halves:

- **East Japuca** — Academy of Supreme Arcane Devastation  
- **West Japuca** — Institute of Responsible Magic Use  

The two sides are separated by:

- A single bridge  
- A shared central area at its base  

---

### Daily Life
The village is built along two hillsides, with:

- Residences and academy buildings on each side  
- A central bridge connecting both halves  
- A bakery located near the bridge  

---

### Rivalry
The two academies maintain an ongoing rivalry.

- Philosophies differ  
- Methods conflict  
- Competition is constant  

---

### The Yearly Competition
Japuca hosts an annual magical competition.

- Participants demonstrate their abilities  
- Spectators throw potatoes at poor performances  
- Rivalries often escalate during the event  

---

## 🟡 Level 2 — Educated Knowledge

### Reputation
Among those familiar with Aethryss:

- Japuca is known for producing capable but unpredictable mages  
- Its contributions to major events are often… inconsistent  
- Its methods are considered unconventional  

---

### Academic Divide
While the two academies claim different approaches:

- One emphasizes power and spectacle  
- The other emphasizes control and responsibility  

In practice, both produce results that vary widely.

---

### Cultural Traits
Japuca’s culture encourages:

- Expression over restraint  
- Competition over cooperation  
- Visibility over subtlety  

---

### Notable Incidents
The village has been involved in multiple events where:

- Outcomes were unexpected  
- Results were exaggerated  
- Damage was… not entirely intentional  

---

## Notes

Japuca is not defined by perfection.

It is defined by how loudly it tries.
