
## Overview
The Imperium of Caelvar is a vast and unified power, composed of multiple kingdoms working together under a shared structure of governance, influence, and purpose.

While each kingdom maintains its own identity and function, all contribute to the strength and stability of the Imperium as a whole.

At its center lies [[Caelvaris, The capital of Caelvar]], a city unlike any other, where political, economic, and strategic power converge.

---

## 🟢 Level 1 — Common Knowledge

### Structure of the Imperium
The Imperium is made up of five major kingdoms:

- [[Kingdom of Valdren]] — Known for its military strength  
- [[Kingdom of Aethryss]] — Known for magic and arcane study  
- [[Kingdom of Viremont]] — Known for trade and commerce  
- [[Kingdom of Elarion]] — Known for nature and agriculture  
- [[Kingdom of Thalmyr]] — Known for faith and religious practices  

Each kingdom fulfills a role that supports the Imperium as a whole.

---

### The Capital
At the heart of the Imperium lies [[Caelvaris, The capital of Caelvar]].

It is said to be as large as an entire kingdom’s worth of cities combined, divided into multiple districts and surrounded by massive walls.

It is where major decisions are made and where influence from all kingdoms converges.

---

### Unity and Tension
The Imperium is seen as strong and stable, but not without internal tension.

Different kingdoms often have differing priorities, and while they remain united, disagreements are not uncommon.

---

### Reputation
To most people, the Imperium represents:

- Order  
- Strength  
- Stability  

It is considered one of the most powerful forces in the known world.

---

## 🟡 Level 2 — Educated Knowledge

### Balance of Power
Power within the Imperium is not centralized in a single ruler or institution.

Instead, it is distributed across:

- Kingdom leadership  
- Influential factions  
- Major institutions  

This creates a balance where no single force fully dominates the others.

---

### Influence of Factions
Across the Imperium, many institutions are influenced by larger ideological forces.

In some regions, such as [[Kingdom of Aethryss]], this is especially visible through the presence of groups like:

- The Grand Circle  
- The Open Hand  

These influences shape how decisions are made within institutions, often creating internal divisions.

---

### The Role of the Capital
While [[Caelvaris, The capital of Caelvar]] is the center of power, it does not directly control every aspect of the Imperium.

Instead, it acts as:

- A political meeting ground  
- A center of coordination  
- A place where influence is negotiated rather than imposed  

---

### Inter-Kingdom Dynamics
The kingdoms of the Imperium are interdependent.

- Valdren provides military security  
- Aethryss advances magical understanding  
- Viremont sustains economic flow  
- Elarion ensures resources and balance with nature  
- Thalmyr maintains spiritual and cultural cohesion  

Despite this, competition and differing ideologies can lead to friction.

---

### External Awareness
Educated individuals are aware that the Imperium is not the only power in the world.

Other lands and forces exist beyond its borders, though knowledge about them is often limited or inconsistent.

---

## Notes
The Imperium is often described not as a single entity, but as a collection of powers moving in alignment—most of the time.
