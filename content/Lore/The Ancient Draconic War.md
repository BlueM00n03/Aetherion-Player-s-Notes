Historical Event  
Arcane Turning Point  

---

## Overview
The Ancient Draconic War was a defining event in the history of the world, marking the end of draconic dominance over magic and the beginning of its widespread use among all races.

It was not a single war.

It was the breaking of an age.

---

## The Age Before

In ancient times, dragons were the primary wielders of magic.

Magic was not studied.

It was spoken.

Through what is now known as **Ancient Draconic**, dragons shaped reality directly.

Other races did not wield magic independently.

They were taught fragments of it.

---

## Proto-Dragons

Before the known dragon lineages, there existed the **Proto-Dragons**.

They were:

- Closest to raw magic in existence  
- Less defined in form and identity 
- Naturally aligned with the flow of arcane forces

They did not cast magic.

They embodied it.

---

### Decline of the Proto-Dragons

As dragonkind evolved and distinctions began to form:

- Proto-dragons became increasingly rare  
- Some vanished entirely  
- Others are believed to have become the foundation of later dragon lineages  

Their true fate remains unknown.

---

## The Divisions

Over time, dragons separated into distinct groups, defined by how they understood and expressed power through Ancient Draconic.

- Chromatic Dragons — Power drawn from the self, expressed through dominance and force  
- Metallic Dragons — Power shaped through structure, discipline, and preservation  
- Gem Dragons — Power refined inward, perfected through mind and focus  
- Warden Dragons — Power amplified through connection, shaped by bonds and those who follow  

These were not simple differences in temperament.

They were fundamentally incompatible approaches to power.

---

## Warden Dragons

Warden Dragons do not differ in their ability to shape reality through Ancient Draconic.

They differ in where that power comes from.

Their words carry greater weight when:

- Spoken among those bound to them  
- Anchored to places or communities  
- Reinforced by trust, loyalty, or shared purpose  

Alone, they remain powerful.

But never at their strongest.

---

## The War

Conflict between these groups escalated into a war that spanned the entire realm.

As it progressed:

- Magic was used without restraint  
- Entire regions were reshaped or erased  
- Arcane forces became unstable  

The war did not remain contained.

It spread into the fabric of reality itself.

---

## Arcane Collapse

The excessive use of magic led to catastrophic consequences:

- Spells destabilized  
- Magical effects persisted beyond control  
- Regions became permanently altered  

Reality itself began to fracture under the strain.

---

## Role of the Warden Dragons

Unlike other groups, Warden Dragons did not act as a unified force.

Their nature made unity difficult.

- Some aligned with Chromatic Dragons  
- Others stood with Metallic Dragons  
- Some withdrew entirely  
- Others fought only to protect what they were bound to  

Their involvement was fragmented.

Personal.

Often contradictory.

---

### Consequence

As the war escalated:

- Bonds were broken  
- Communities destroyed  
- Followers lost  

For Warden Dragons, this was not only loss.

It was weakening.

Many fell not through direct defeat—

But through isolation.

---

## Divine Intervention

As the conflict reached its peak, divine forces intervened.

Not to choose a victor.

But to prevent total collapse.

---

### The Judgment

The gods acted to:

- Halt the war  
- Stabilize reality  
- Prevent further destruction  

---

### The Draconic Deities

At the same time, the draconic deities of each group took decisive action.

They removed part of the innate magical nature of dragons.

---

## The Redistribution

Magic was no longer the domain of dragons.

It was shared.

- Across races  
- Across cultures  
- Across the world  

What was once instinct became:

- Study  
- Practice  
- Discipline  

---

## The Language of Magic

Ancient Draconic, once the natural expression of magic, became:

- The foundation of spellcasting  
- The basis for incantations and evocations  

Modern draconic languages still exist.

But they are no longer the same.

---

## Present Day

Today:

- Magic is widely practiced  
- Its origins are only partially understood  
- Its connection to dragons is known—but distant  

Some scholars dedicate their lives to studying:

- Ancient Draconic  
- Pre-War spellforms  
- Proto-draconic theory  

---

## Lasting Effects

- Regions still bear scars of the war  
- Magic remains powerful, but imperfect  
- Dragons are no longer what they once were  

---

## Notes

The Ancient Draconic War is not remembered as a victory.

It is remembered as a warning.
