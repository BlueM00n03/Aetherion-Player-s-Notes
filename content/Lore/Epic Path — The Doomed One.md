
You come from a distinguished family with a long and storied lineage, one tied to ancient powers and forgotten pacts. However, you had the misfortune of being born under a fell astrological sign—one that portended a doom of epic proportions. Your parents died shortly after you were born, under mysterious circumstances.

All your life you have been plagued by ill luck, and you have long suspected that your death will be as ugly as it is spectacular. The priests and seers of the [[Kingdom of Thalmyr]] make signs against evil when they see you, and you have often been treated with prejudice and superstitious dread.

Nevertheless, you have learned that your fate is not yet sealed. There are those who believe your doom can be avoided—or at least understood. Somewhere, someone knows what awaits you… and what might protect you from it.

---

## Adventure Hook
There are those who believe that your doom reflects something far greater than yourself.

If you can save yourself—

You may be able to save something much larger.

---

## Heroic Tasks

- To defeat the creature that has been fated to kill you.  
- To forge the one artifact that will protect you from your doom. These items provide resistance to all damage that originates from the creature that is fated to kill you. Choose one from the following list:

› Robe of the Archmagi  
› Breastplate of Invulnerability  
› Cloak of Invisibility

## Divine Blessing

### Blessing of Protection
You gain a +1 bonus to your AC and to all saving throws.
