
## Overview
The Kingdom of Elarion is the verdant heart of the [[Imperium of Caelvar]], defined by its deep connection to nature, sustainability, and balance.

Where others seek to shape the world, Elarion seeks to live within it.

---

## 🟢 Level 1 — Common Knowledge

### Identity
Elarion is known for its natural landscapes and agricultural strength.

- Vast forests and fertile lands  
- Close ties to nature  
- Communities built in harmony with their surroundings  

---

### Reputation
To outsiders, Elarion is seen as:

- Peaceful  
- Grounded  
- Traditional  

It is often viewed as the most stable and self-sufficient of the kingdoms.

---

### Resources
Elarion provides:

- Food and natural resources  
- Herbal knowledge and natural remedies  
- Materials essential to the rest of the Imperium  

---

### Role in the Imperium
Elarion ensures:

- Sustained resource flow  
- Environmental balance  
- Long-term stability  

---

## 🟡 Level 2 — Educated Knowledge

### Philosophy of Balance
Elarion values:

- Sustainability over expansion  
- Harmony over dominance  
- Patience over urgency  

This philosophy influences both daily life and larger decisions.

---

### Controlled Growth
Unlike other kingdoms, Elarion does not expand rapidly.

- Development is deliberate  
- Resources are carefully managed  
- Overuse is avoided whenever possible  

---

### Relationship with Other Kingdoms
Elarion maintains necessary connections, but remains cautious.

- Supplies Viremont with resources  
- Supports the Imperium’s needs  
- Often disagrees with excessive exploitation or expansion  

---

### Use of Magic
Magic in Elarion is often tied to nature.

- Used to support growth and healing  
- Less focused on raw power or experimentation  
- Viewed as part of a larger natural system  

---

## Notes
Elarion does not resist change.

It simply refuses to rush it.
