## Overview
The Kingdom of Aethryss is the arcane heart of the [[Imperium of Caelvar]], known for its pursuit of magical knowledge, study, and application.

It is a land where magic is not only practiced, but constantly debated, refined, and challenged.

---

## 🟢 Level 1 — Common Knowledge

### Identity
Aethryss is widely known as the center of magical learning within the Imperium.

- Home to many arcane institutions  
- Attracts scholars, mages, and researchers  
- Magic is part of everyday life  

---

### Reputation
To outsiders, Aethryss is seen as:

- Intelligent  
- Mysterious  
- Occasionally dangerous  

Magic is respected—but also feared.

---

### Institutions
The kingdom is filled with academies, research centers, and magical organizations.

One of the most well-known is the [[Cyrion Enclave]].

---

### Role in the Imperium
Aethryss provides:

- Magical advancement  
- Knowledge and research  
- Support in arcane matters  

---

## 🟡 Level 2 — Educated Knowledge

### Divided Ideology
Aethryss is shaped by two major ideological forces:

- The Grand Circle — advocates structure, regulation, and control of magic  
- The Open Hand — believes magic is a tool defined by those who use it  

These influences are present across most institutions in the kingdom.

---

### Institutional Balance
No single institution defines Aethryss.

- Multiple organizations operate independently  
- Influence is shared and contested  
- Even within institutions, perspectives may differ  

---

### The Cyrion Enclave
The [[Cyrion Enclave]] is one of several major institutions.

It is particularly known for:

- Threat assessment  
- Study of unstable or dangerous magic  
- Advisory roles in high-risk situations  

Its general stance tends toward caution, though not all within it agree.

---

### Approach to Magic
Magic in Aethryss is not blindly trusted.

It is:

- Studied  
- Questioned  
- Controlled in some cases  

Different scholars—and factions—disagree on how far that control should go.

---

## Notes
Aethryss is not defined by magic alone—

But by how differently its people believe magic should be used.
