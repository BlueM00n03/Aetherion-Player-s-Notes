You once had something real.

Someone who mattered.

A connection that grounded you—something that made everything else make sense.

Then one day, everything changed.

A moment. An event. Something went wrong.

Since then, nothing has been the same.

Aelira still exists.

But not as she was.

Something altered her—something tied to the mind, to dreams, or to something beyond both. The person you knew is still there… somewhere. But what remains is fractured, distorted, or incomplete.

Others may accept what she has become.

You do not.

You know something was taken. Changed. Corrupted.

And you will not accept that this is how her story ends.

You will find out what happened.

You will understand it.

And if there is any way to bring her back—

You will take it.

No matter the cost.

---

## Adventure Hook

There are forces that shape dreams, thoughts, and identity.

If something has been altered within the mind—

It may not be lost.

It may still exist… somewhere.

---

## Heroic Tasks

- To understand what happened to [[Aelira Veyne]].  
- To uncover the force or event that altered her.  
- To find a way to restore her—or accept what she has become.  

Additionally, recover one lesser and one greater artifact tied to your connection, your mind, or the forces that changed her:

› **(Lesser) Ring of Protection**: a token tied to a memory you refuse to let go.  
› **(Lesser) Amulet of Health**: something that stabilizes body or mind against corruption.  
› **(Lesser) Ioun Stone (Awareness)**: a fragment of clarity that helps you perceive what others cannot.  

› **(Greater) Helm of Brilliance**: a focus capable of piercing illusion or distortion.  
› **(Greater) Dancing Sword**: a weapon that responds to intent or thought.  
› **(Greater) Rod of Absorption**: a tool capable of interacting with unstable magical forces.
