
## Overview
Caelvaris is the capital of the [[Imperium of Caelvar]] and its largest city, serving as the central point of political, economic, and strategic power.

It is not merely a city.

It is a convergence of everything the Imperium represents.

---

## 🟢 Level 1 — Common Knowledge

### Scale
Caelvaris is vast.

- Said to rival the size of entire kingdoms’ cities combined  
- Surrounded by massive outer walls  
- Divided into multiple districts  

It is unlike any other place in the Imperium.

---

### Identity
Caelvaris is known as:

- The seat of power  
- A hub of activity  
- A place where all kingdoms intersect  

People from across the Imperium can be found within its walls.

---

### Districts
The city is divided into seven major districts.

Each serves a different purpose, including:

- Governance  
- Trade  
- Military presence  
- Religious centers  
- Residential areas  

---

### Reputation
To most, Caelvaris is:

- Impressive  
- Overwhelming  
- Full of opportunity  

It is a place where anything can happen.

---

## 🟡 Level 2 — Educated Knowledge

### Center of Influence
Caelvaris is not just politically important—it is where influence is negotiated.

- Representatives from all kingdoms are present  
- Decisions are rarely simple or immediate  
- Power is often subtle rather than direct  

---

### Layered Authority
Authority in Caelvaris is complex.

- Multiple factions operate within the city  
- Institutions overlap in influence  
- Control is shared, not absolute  

---

### The District System
Each district functions almost like its own city.

- Governed differently  
- Influenced by different powers  
- Serving distinct roles within the whole  

Movement between districts can mean entering entirely different environments.

---

### Beneath the Surface
While the surface of Caelvaris represents order and unity, it is widely understood that:

- Not all activity is visible  
- Influence exists beyond official channels  
- Information is as valuable as power  

---

## Notes
Caelvaris is not defined by what it shows—

But by what it contains.
