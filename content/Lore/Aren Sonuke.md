## Overview
Aren Sonuke is a mage from the village of [[Japuca]] and the twin brother of [[Arue Sonuke]].

Sharing similar magical ability but a very different outlook, Aren represents a more controlled and precise approach to spellcasting.

---

## Origin

### Japuca
Like his sister, Aren was raised in [[Japuca]], a village known for its competitive magical culture and opposing academies.

---

### Education
Aren is associated with:

- **West Japuca — Institute of Responsible Magic Use**

He favors:

- Precision  
- Control  
- Efficiency  

---

## Relationship with Arue

Aren and Arue share:

- Comparable magical ability  
- A constant rivalry  
- A long history of competition  

Their interactions are defined by opposing views:

- Arue values power and spectacle  
- Aren values refinement and execution  

---

## The Yearly Competition

During a major competition in Japuca:

- Aren reached the final round  
- Faced Arue in a duel  

The match escalated until:

- A spell struck nearby gunpowder kegs  
- The resulting explosion destroyed the arena  

---

## The Aftermath

Following the incident:

- Both siblings were taken from Japuca  
- They were separated to prevent further escalation  
- Placed under supervision within the Imperium  

---

## Personality

### Core Traits
- Confident  
- Competitive  
- Focused  

---

### Beliefs
Aren believes:

- Control is superior to raw power  
- Precision defines true mastery  
- Discipline is what separates a mage from chaos  

---

### Behavior
- Prefers calculated spell use  
- Avoids unnecessary risk  
- Responds to challenges directly  

---

## Motivation

Aren seeks to:

- Prove his superiority over Arue  
- Demonstrate the value of controlled magic  
- Establish himself as a master of precision  

---

## Notes

Aren does not seek spectacle.

He seeks perfection.

---

## Quote
