
## Overview
Arue Sonuke is a mage from the village of [[Japuca]], located within the [[Kingdom of Aethryss]].

Known for her overwhelming confidence and destructive magical tendencies, she seeks to become the ultimate spellcaster and bring recognition and glory to her homeland.

---

## Origin

### Japuca
Japuca is a village known for producing mages.

- Located in a valley  
- Split into two opposing schools  
- Built across two hills connected by a single bridge  

---

### The Two Schools

- **East Japuca** — Academy of Supreme Arcane Devastation  
- **West Japuca** — Institute of Responsible Magic Use  

Despite their proximity, the two institutions maintain a strong rivalry.

---

### Culture
Japuca is known for:

- Intense magical competition  
- Frequent destructive incidents  
- A yearly event that often ends in chaos  

---

## The Yearly Competition

Each year, Japuca hosts a major magical competition.

- Participants compete to demonstrate magical skill  
- Spectators throw potatoes at poor performances  
- Rivalries between schools often escalate  

---

### The Incident
During one such competition:

- Arue defeated all other competitors  
- She then faced her twin brother, [[Aren Sonuke]]  

During their duel, a spell went off course and struck a set of stored gunpowder kegs near the arena.

The resulting explosion:

- Destroyed the arena  
- Scattered the village’s potato reserves  
- Caused significant disruption across the area  

The exact details remain… debated.

---

## The Aftermath

Following the event:

- Authorities from the capital intervened  
- Arue and her brother were separated  
- Both were taken away from Japuca  

---

### Current Status
Arue now operates under supervision within the Imperium.

She has been given responsibilities intended to:

- Direct her abilities  
- Prevent further uncontrolled destruction
