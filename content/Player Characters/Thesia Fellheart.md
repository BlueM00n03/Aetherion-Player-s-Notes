

## Overview
Thesia Fellhart was born into [[House Fellhart]], an ancient lineage tied to the religious and prophetic traditions of [[Kingdom of Thalmyr]].

Her life has been marked by a persistent and undeniable pattern of misfortune—one that has followed her since birth and shaped both how she is perceived and how she sees the world.

Where others would resist such a fate, she has chosen something else entirely:

To move forward regardless.

---

## Background

### Origin
House Fellhart was once a respected lineage within Thalmyr.

- Not among the most powerful  
- But among the oldest  
- Known for deep ties to faith and prophecy  

Their blood is said to carry:

- Remnants of long forgotten divine pacts  

---

### The Night of Her Birth
On the night she was born:

- The skies were wrong  
- Constellations appeared misaligned  
- Signs pointed toward a fracture in destiny  

Within hours:

- Both of her parents died under unexplained circumstances  

---

### The Curse
From that moment onward, her life was defined by misfortune.

- If something could go wrong, it would  
- Accidents followed her  
- Failure was constant  

Within Thalmyr:

- Some priests declared it a curse  
- Others chose to avoid her entirely  

---

### Interpretation
Her condition was never fully understood.

Some believed:

- She was marked by fate  

Others believed:

- She was a disruption to it  

---

## The Pact

### Decision
Tired of inevitability, she sought something beyond fate itself.

She turned to a fiendish entity—one that does not follow destiny.

---

### Terms of the Pact
The agreement was simple:

- Remove her bad luck  
- Free her from the fate that awaited her  

---

### Outcome
The entity did not lie.

But it did not tell the full truth.

---

## The Truth

Her bad luck was not removed.

It was given direction.

---

### Realization
What once seemed random is now understood to be:

- Intentional  
- Consistent  
- Leading somewhere  

Every failure, accident, and disaster is not coincidence.

It is adjustment.

---

## Patron

### [[The Collector of Destinies]]

An entity that interferes with prophecy itself.

To it:

- Prophecies are pieces  
- Heroes are variables  
- Gods are predictable players  

---

### Role of the Character
She is not simply cursed.

She is:

- A piece that should not exist  
- A disruption in a larger system  
- A variable capable of altering an outcome  

---

## Enemy

### Divine Agent
A being aligned with divine forces has been tasked with eliminating her.

- Sees her existence as a flaw  
- Believes her survival creates imbalance  
- Acts to restore what it considers the correct path  

---

### Possible Origin
This agent is believed to be connected to:

- Higher powers tied to [[Kingdom of Thalmyr]]  

---

## Personality

### Core Traits
- Absurdly positive  
- Accepts failure as inevitable  
- Does not resist misfortune  
- Finds meaning in chaos  

---

### Behavior
She does not try to avoid things going wrong.

She assumes they will.

And moves forward anyway.

---

### Outlook
- Calm in the face of consequences  
- Unbothered by setbacks  
- Sees failure as part of a larger process  

---
