## Overview
Braern Loraxalim was raised within [[House Loraxalim]], a minor but highly respected house known for carrying out sensitive operations within the Imperium.

From an early age, his life was defined by discipline, training, and purpose.

---

## Background

### House Loraxalim
House Loraxalim is a small but respected house known for:

- Discretion  
- Efficiency  
- Carrying out tasks others avoid  

It does not seek attention.

It fulfills its role.

---

### Upbringing
Braern did not have a traditional childhood.

From the age of six:

- He was trained  
- Conditioned  
- Prepared for service  

His purpose was simple:

To serve the house.

---

### Service
Braern carried out his duties with exceptional skill.

- Missions completed without trace  
- Targets removed cleanly  
- Orders followed without hesitation  

---

## The Incident

One day, something changed.

- His family no longer recognized him  
- His name meant nothing to them  
- His existence was denied  

---

### Aftermath

- Records of him are missing or altered  
- Those who should remember him do not  
- His past exists only in his own memory  

---

## Current State

Braern now moves through the Imperium as:

- A stranger to his own past  
- A man without recognition  
- A question without an answer  

---

## Personality

### Core Traits
- Controlled  
- Observant  
- Distrustful  

---

### Beliefs
Braern believes:

- Trust must be earned  
- Loyalty is fragile  
- Truth can be taken  

---

### Relationship with Magic
Braern does not distrust magic entirely.

But he holds deep hatred for:

- Magic that alters memory  

To him:

> “To erase without a blade is cowardice.”

---

## Motivation

Braern seeks to:

- Understand what was done to him  
- Discover who is responsible  
- Reclaim what was taken  

---

## Notes

Braern remembers everything.

Even if no one else does.

---

## Quote

> “I was there.  
> That should be enough.”
