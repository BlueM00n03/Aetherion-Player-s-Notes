Celestial Warlock

## Overview
Caelum Soryn has never known a silent mind.

From his earliest memories there was always a presence beside his thoughts — calm, patient, and impossibly old. That presence was [[Ithryss]], a quori who long ago turned away from the tyranny of Dal Quor.

Unlike many of his kind, Ithryss did not seek domination or control. Instead, he watched, listened, and waited for a mind capable of sharing the quiet space between thoughts. In Caelum, he found someone who listened.

Ithryss never commanded or controlled him. He simply walked beside Caelum within the shared space of his mind, offering questions instead of answers and patience instead of certainty.

Through that quiet companionship, Caelum grew into a thoughtful and observant man — one who preferred understanding over power, and reflection over haste.

---

## Early Life
Caelum was raised within the [[Imperium of Caelvar|The Imperium of Caelvar]], in the [[Kingdom of Aethryss]], where magic is studied, regulated, and carefully observed.

He spent much of his early life within the influence of the [[Cyrion Enclave]], a respected arcane institution known for studying unusual magical phenomena and maintaining strict oversight of anything deemed unstable.

To most, Caelum was simply another quiet student.

To a few, he was something… worth watching.

---

## Aelira
Despite the strangeness of his mind, Caelum once believed he could live a simple life.

For a time, he stood between two worlds: the one shaped by ancient whispers within his thoughts, and the ordered, disciplined structure of the Imperium.

In that world, he found something rare — love.

Her name was [[Aelira Veyne]], an apprentice archivist tied loosely to the lesser noble [[House Veyne]], a minor but well-connected family operating within [[Caelvaris, The capital of Caelvar]].

With her, the world felt uncomplicated. She did not see him as a Kalashtar, nor as something to be studied or understood.

To her, he was simply Caelum.

For a while, that was enough.

---

## The Incident
That peace ended when their path crossed with something tied to the Plane of Dreams, a presence that had slipped through unseen cracks — something even the scholars of [[Kingdom of Aethryss|Aethryss]] had failed to detect.

The encounter was swift, silent, and devastating.

In the psychic struggle that followed, Aelira’s mind was shattered — her memories and emotions torn apart like fragments of glass by a force neither of them fully understood.

She survived.

But the person she had been was lost.

---

## Aftermath
The incident was quietly contained.

Records were sealed.

Aelira was taken into the care of individuals connected to both [[House Veyne]] and certain officials within [[Caelvaris, The capital of Caelvar]], her condition classified as a matter of arcane sensitivity.

Caelum was left with questions.

And silence.

---

## Turning Point
That moment changed him forever.

The weight of his hesitation became something he could not ignore — not guilt that consumed him, but a quiet understanding that inaction carries its own cost.

In the years that followed, another presence revealed itself — a distant celestial patron, drawn not by destiny, but perhaps by the quiet resolve Caelum carried.

The pact that formed was not one of ambition or purpose imposed from above, but of choice.

Caelum accepted that power not to change the Imperium, nor to become something greater than he was, but simply to ensure that when faced with darkness again, he would not stand still.

---

## Present Day
Now Caelum walks the Imperium as a quiet observer.

No longer tied to [[Kingdom of Aethryss]], yet shaped by it, he travels between kingdoms where knowledge, people, and circumstance take him.

[[Ithryss]] remains beside him in the silent spaces of his mind, offering the patient wisdom of centuries, while the faint warmth of celestial power guides his magic.

He does not claim to be a hero.

He is simply a man trying to do better than he once did.

And perhaps, one day, understand what truly happened to [[Aelira Veyne]].
