
# The Collector of Destinies

## What [[Thesia Fellheart|You]] Know

You made a pact with something that does not follow destiny.

Not a god.  
Not a fiend in the usual sense.  

Something else.

---

## The Pact

You asked for:

- Your bad luck to be removed  
- Your fate to be changed  

And it agreed.

---

## What You Realized

Nothing was removed.

Instead—

Everything gained meaning.

---

### Your “Luck”

Things still go wrong.

But they are no longer random.

- Failures happen at specific moments  
- Accidents push you in certain directions  
- Outcomes feel… guided  

As if something is adjusting the path beneath your feet.

---

## The Presence

It does not speak often.

But when it does, it does not give answers.

It gives:

- Observations  
- Questions  
- Possibilities  

It never tells you what to do.

But it makes you think about what will happen.

---

## What It Is (As You Understand It)

You do not see it as a master.

You see it as:

- Something watching the game  
- Something that moves pieces  
- Something that changed where you stand  

---

## What It Wants

You are not entirely sure.

But you feel:

- You matter to it  
- Your choices are important  
- Your path is… unusual  

---

## What You Are

You are not free from fate.

You are not bound to it either.

You are something in between.

---

## What You Feel

- You are being guided, not controlled  
- You are being tested, not protected  
- You are being watched, not judged  

---

## Your Belief

> “If this goes wrong…  
> it’s because it needs to.”

---

## Important

You do not feel owned.

You chose this.

And you would choose it again.

---

## Notes

You do not know the full truth.

But you know one thing for certain:

Nothing that happens to you is meaningless anymore.

---

## Quote

> “It didn’t take my fate away.  
> It made it matter.”
